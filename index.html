HTML markup formatter

Paste raw HTML or upload an HTML file. The formatter changes whitespace and indentation only as far as practical. It does not render the HTML, repair invalid markup, reorder attributes, change quote styles, or insert missing tags.

Nothing you paste or upload leaves your browser.
